# AI assurance review checklist

1. Name an accountable owner for each safety-critical decision.
2. Record assumptions about operators, response time and operating context.
3. Test whether a human can realistically detect, understand and recover.
4. Log overrides, disabled safeguards and the authority that approved them.
5. Define pre-deployment evidence thresholds and stop conditions.
6. Monitor distribution shift, near misses and weak signals after deployment.
7. Give an independent reviewer access to evidence, not only summary metrics.
8. Maintain an incident path that preserves logs and prevents blame diffusion.
