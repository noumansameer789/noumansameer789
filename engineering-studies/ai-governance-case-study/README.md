# AI governance case study: 2018 autonomous-vehicle fatality

An MSc governance portfolio examining the 2018 Uber autonomous-vehicle fatality
through Fast Track and Process-Based Governance lenses.

## Core argument

The failure cannot be reduced to one classifier error. The safety chain combined
diluted accountability, disabled or weakened safeguards, unreliable
human-in-the-loop monitoring and an operating context that made timely recovery
unrealistic.

## Evidence standard

- Separate documented facts from inference.
- Attribute every chronology claim to a primary investigation or official record.
- Distinguish model performance, system safety and governance accountability.
- Avoid publishing private course materials or assessor feedback.

`docs/review-checklist.md` provides a reusable assurance checklist for future
AI systems. The full assessed essay is intentionally not published.
